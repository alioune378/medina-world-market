    Our Story

    One address on S Hamilton Road, two reasons to stop: a market stocked with the ingredients of many homes, and a kitchen cooking the dishes that go with them.

    Medina World Market & Restaurant has served Columbus's African and international communities since 2012. Shoppers come for rice by the bag, halal meats, spices and familiar brands; diners come for plates from the grill and stews that take their time.

    This page will carry Medina's own story — the founders, the kitchen's signature dishes, the people behind the counter — written with the owner.

    What launch requires

    Payments

    Hosted Square or Stripe checkout, so card data never touches the site.

    Inventory

    Catalogue synced from the point-of-sale system or a simple admin, with stock and shipping eligibility per product.

    Order alerts

    New orders pushed to a counter tablet with sound and SMS backup; customers texted at each step.

    Shipping

    Live USPS/UPS/FedEx rates through a serverless function and a rating service such as Shippo or EasyPost.

    Taxes

    Ohio and Franklin County rules for prepared food versus groceries, confirmed with Medina's accountant.

    Accounts & consent

    Optional sign-in for favorites and reorders; recorded opt-ins, STOP handling, one-click unsubscribe.
